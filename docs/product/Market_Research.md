# Executive Summary

TACTIX enters an emerging market where football is the most followed sport in Morocco, yet there is no dominant fantasy football platform dedicated to the Botola Pro Inwi.

While millions of Moroccan fans consume football through television, social media, sports websites, and messaging platforms, their interaction with domestic football remains largely passive. Existing fantasy football experiences focus almost exclusively on European competitions such as the Premier League.

TACTIX aims to bridge this gap by delivering a modern, premium fantasy football experience centered on Moroccan football, creating year-round engagement through competition, strategy, and community.

Fantasy sports have evolved from niche hobby games into one of the largest segments of digital sports entertainment.

Successful platforms include:

- Sorare
- Dream11
- ESPN Fantasy
- Yahoo Fantasy
- Sleeper

Key industry trends:

- Real-time scoring

- Personalized statistics

The success of these platforms demonstrates that fans enjoy participating in sports, not merely watching them.

# Moroccan Football Market

Football is the most popular sport in Morocco.

Major competitions include:

- Botola Pro Inwi
- Botola 2
- Throne Cup
- CAF Champions League
- CAF Confederation Cup
- Africa Cup of Nations
- FIFA World Cup

The achievements of the Moroccan national team have significantly increased domestic interest in football and digital engagement.

Supporters actively follow:

- Raja Club Athletic
- Wydad Athletic Club
- AS FAR
- RS Berkane
- FUS Rabat
- Maghreb de Fès
- Olympic Safi
- Hassania Agadir
- Renaissance Zemamra
- COD Meknès
- and other Botola clubs.

This growing enthusiasm creates favorable conditions for a fantasy football platform.

# Key Takeaways

The Moroccan football ecosystem presents a significant opportunity for a dedicated fantasy football platform.

No major competitor currently offers a comprehensive fantasy experience focused exclusively on Botola Pro Inwi.

TACTIX can establish itself as the leading football engagement platform by combining fantasy gameplay, live statistics, strategic decision-making, and community features.

The long-term opportunity extends beyond fantasy football, positioning TACTIX as the digital home for Moroccan football supporters and, eventually, football fans across Africa.
