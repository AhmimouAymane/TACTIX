# 1. Objective

The Business Model defines how TACTIX generates sustainable revenue while keeping the fantasy game fair, free-to-play, and accessible.

Revenue comes from a free tier supported by advertising and a paid Premium tier offering enhanced insights and experience.

---

# 2. Business Principles

- The core fantasy game is always free
- Premium never provides gameplay advantages
- No pay-to-win mechanics
- Ads must never disrupt gameplay
- Revenue supports product quality
- Monetization is transparent to users

# 4. Monetization Strategy

## Two-Tier Model

Free

Supported by ads.

Premium

Subscription with enhanced experience.

## Strategy Rationale

- Large free base drives engagement
- Ads monetize the casual majority
- Premium monetizes the engaged minority
- Fairness preserved across tiers

---

# 5. Free Tier

## Includes

- All core fantasy features
- Squad creation and management
- Transfers and captain selection
- Public and private leagues
- Live scoring
- Basic player statistics
- Basic news content

## Supported By

- Banner ads
- Interstitial ads (limited)
- Sponsored content (labeled)

---

# 6. Premium Tier

## Includes

All free features, plus:

- Ad-free experience
- Advanced player statistics
- TACTIX AI insights
- Fixture difficulty rankings
- Advanced comparison tools
- Premium news content
- Early access to new features
- Priority support

## Excludes

- No gameplay advantages
- No extra points
- No exclusive players
- No transfer benefits

# 8. Advertising Model

## Ad Formats

Banner ads

Interstitial ads (gameweek transitions only)

Sponsored content (clearly labeled)

Native ads (Discover feed)

## Ad Rules

- No ads during live scoring screens
- No ads during deadline countdown
- Maximum 2 interstitials per session
- All ads are skippable
- Ads must not mislead users
- Ad-free for Premium users

## Ad Providers

Mobile ad mediation platforms.

Compliance with store ad policies.

# 14. Payment Rails

## In-App Payments

- Android: Google Play Billing
- iOS: Apple App Store In-App Purchase

## Constraints

- Morocco's payment ecosystem relies primarily on cards
- Store billing handles cards, carrier billing, and local methods where available
- TACTIX does not store card data
- No external payment processor in Version 1

---

# 15. Financial Risks

## Risk: Low conversion

Mitigation: Trial, clear value communication, feature teasers.

## Risk: High churn

Mitigation: Annual plan, engagement features, retention programs.

## Risk: Ad revenue below expectations

Mitigation: Sponsorship and partnership pipeline.

## Risk: Store commissions

Mitigation: Pricing accounts for 15-30% store fees.

## Risk: Currency volatility

Mitigation: Regional pricing review each season.

# 17. Acceptance Criteria

✓ Free tier provides full gameplay.

✓ Premium offers clear value without pay-to-win.

✓ Ads are non-disruptive and skippable.

✓ Pricing is defined and localized.

✓ Store billing is integrated.

✓ No card data is stored.

✓ Sponsorships are labeled and fair.

✓ Financial targets are tracked.
