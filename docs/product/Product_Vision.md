# Product Vision - TACTIX

> Version: 1.0
> Product Name: TACTIX
> Tagline: Master the Game.
> Initial Competition: Botola Pro Inwi
> Platform: Android & iOS (Flutter)
> Last Updated: August 2026

# Executive Summary

TACTIX is a premium fantasy football platform designed to revolutionize how supporters engage with Moroccan football. Initially launching with Botola Pro Inwi, TACTIX enables fans to become virtual football managers by building squads composed of real Botola players and earning points based on their real-life performances.

Unlike traditional sports applications that focus only on scores or news, TACTIX transforms every match into a strategic experience. Every goal, assist, save, and tactical decision directly influences a user's fantasy team, encouraging continuous engagement throughout the season.

TACTIX is built as a scalable football ecosystem. While the first release focuses exclusively on Botola Pro Inwi, the platform is architected to support additional national and international competitions in future releases without requiring a complete redesign.

The long-term ambition is to become the leading fantasy football platform in North Africa and eventually a comprehensive football companion that combines fantasy gaming, statistics, predictions, AI-powered insights, community features, and official league partnerships.

# Vision Statement

To become the most trusted and engaging fantasy football platform in North Africa, transforming how supporters experience football by combining strategy, competition, and technology into one premium digital ecosystem.

# Mission Statement

TACTIX exists to deepen the connection between football supporters and the game they love. By providing an intuitive, competitive, and data-driven fantasy experience, we empower every fan to think, plan, and compete like a professional football manager.

Our mission is to make every match meaningful, every decision strategic, and every gameweek unforgettable.

# Product Philosophy

TACTIX is built on one simple belief:

Football should be experienced, not just watched.

Every match should present new strategic opportunities.
Every player should have fantasy value.
Every supporter should feel involved until the final whistle.

TACTIX transforms football supporters into managers, analysts, and competitors by rewarding knowledge, consistency, and smart decision-making.

The platform values fairness over chance, strategy over luck, and long-term engagement over short-term excitement.

Every feature introduced into TACTIX must answer one question:

"Does this make football more engaging for the user?"

If the answer is no, the feature should not be built.
